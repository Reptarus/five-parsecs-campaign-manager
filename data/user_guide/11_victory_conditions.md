# Chapter 11: Victory Conditions

> **Quick Start** (for tabletop veterans)
> - Select one Victory Condition at campaign creation, or none (Core Rules p.64)
> - 17 conditions: turns, Quests, battles won, Unique Individuals killed, character upgrades, and turns on a harder difficulty mode
> - Once selected it cannot be changed, and only that condition counts (Core Rules p.64)
> - Completing a campaign with a condition awards an Elite Rank (Core Rules p.65)
> - Progress is tracked automatically on the Dashboard

## Overview

A Victory Condition gives a campaign a distinct goal. Campaigns may otherwise go on indefinitely, and you can leave the choice empty. You select it during campaign creation, and the app tracks progress throughout your campaign.

## Choosing Victory Conditions

During campaign creation (Step 1: Configuration) you may select **one** Victory Condition (Core Rules p.64). Tapping another card replaces your choice. Some goals are intentionally a grind, and some are heavily luck-based. Once the campaign starts, the condition cannot be changed or added.

On Easy mode only **Play 20 Campaign Turns** and **Win 20 Tabletop Battles** are allowed (the Easy rule shown in the app).

### The 17 Conditions

| Group | Conditions |
|-------|-----------|
| Turns | Play 20, 50 or 100 campaign turns |
| Quests | Complete 3, 5 or 10 Quests |
| Battles | Win 20, 50 or 100 tabletop battles |
| Unique Individuals | Kill 10 or 25 Unique Individuals |
| Character upgrades | Upgrade a single character 10 times; upgrade 3 characters 10 times; upgrade 5 characters 10 times |
| Difficulty | Play 50 campaign turns in Challenging, Hardcore or Insanity mode |

For the character upgrade conditions, the characters do not have to be in the crew at the same time: if one character upgrades 10 times and dies, all 10 upgrades still count (Core Rules p.64).

### Custom Victory Conditions

The app also offers **+ Custom Victory Condition** with adjusted targets. A custom condition is an app feature and is not in the rulebook.

### Elite Ranks

Selecting and completing a Victory Condition awards an Elite Rank, once per condition. Elite Ranks carry between campaigns and grant benefits to new ones: a bonus story point and 2 XP per rank, an extra starting character pool for every 3 ranks, and a "Stars of the Story" option usable twice for every 5 ranks (Core Rules p.65).

## Tracking Progress

The Campaign Dashboard's progress strip shows your condition as **Victory:** followed by the condition and its count, for example *Victory: Complete 3 Quests 1 / 3*. The End Phase summary at the end of each turn shows the same count in its **VICTORY PROGRESS** card. Both read the counter the app uses to decide the win, so a Quest condition counts finished Quests, not battles. With no condition picked, neither shows a Victory line.

## Winning and Ending

When the count reaches the target:

1. Press **Save Campaign** in the End Phase. The app awards your Elite Rank (Core Rules p.65) and files the campaign in the Hall of Fame as a victory. Each condition awards an Elite Rank only once.
2. Then choose:
   - **Continue to Next Cycle** to keep playing past your victory, or
   - **Retire the Crew (End Campaign)** to end the campaign there (Core Rules p.8). This cannot be undone.

### Legacy System

A campaign is filed in the Hall of Fame once: when it is won, when you retire the crew, or on the first save once 20 campaign turns are done. The record keeps the crew roster, turns survived, story points, credits held and how the campaign ended (victory, retired or ended).
