# Chapter 18: Battle Simulator

> **Quick Start** (for tabletop veterans)
> - A standalone battle with no campaign: Main Menu > **Battle Simulator**.
> - Pick crew size, opposition, mission and difficulty. The screen shows the crew, enemy force and mission it has rolled.
> - **LAUNCH THIS BATTLE** plays exactly what is on screen, using the same battle screen as a campaign.
> - Nothing from a Simulator battle is saved. Your campaigns are not read or changed.

## Overview

The Battle Simulator lets you run one Five Parsecs battle without creating or loading a campaign. It is useful for learning the battle screen, trying an enemy type, or playing a quick one-off game. It rolls a crew, an opposing force and a mission for you, then opens the same battle companion described in {{chapter:06}}.

The Simulator is a companion like the rest of the app. It tells you what is on the table and what happens each round. You still move miniatures and roll or enter dice yourself.

## Starting the Simulator

Open the Main Menu and tap **Battle Simulator**. The setup screen opens. **< Back to Menu** at the top returns you to the Main Menu.

## The Setup Screen

The setup screen is a single page of four cards. The battle is rolled as soon as the screen opens, and it is rolled again every time you change a setting. What you see in the previews is the battle you will fight.

### Your Crew

- **Crew Size.** A number box for the size of the crew. The enemy numbers depend on it (Core Rules p.63).
- **Roll Again.** Rolls a new crew, opposition and mission using the settings as they stand.

The crew preview lists each member's profile and weapon. The Simulator crew uses the baseline Human profile and weapons rolled on the Core Rules starting-weapon tables. If the Deployment Condition makes some characters sit out, the preview says so.

### Opposition

- **Category.** "Random (roll the encounter table)" or a specific enemy category.
- **Enemy Type.** Rolled with the category, or a specific type from it. Choosing a new category resets the type.

The enemy preview shows the force as generated: how many figures, its make-up and the type's profile.

### Mission

- **Type.** "Random" or a specific mission type.

The mission preview shows the objective, the Deployment Condition and any Notable Sight the battle opens with (Core Rules pp.88-89).

### Difficulty

- **Mode.** The five difficulty modes from the Core Rules (pp.64-65), listed by name.

A note under the dropdown says what the chosen mode changes in a battle. Campaign-only effects, such as XP and Story Points, are left out because the Simulator has no campaign.

### Launch

Press **LAUNCH THIS BATTLE**. The battle starts with the crew, enemy and mission shown on screen. Nothing is re-rolled on launch.

## The Battle

The battle screen fills the whole display. It is the same screen a campaign uses, so everything in {{chapter:06}} applies: setup, the battle rounds, the three tracking tiers and the results drawer.

The Simulator does not read or change any campaign. Even if you have campaigns saved, a Simulator battle does not use their rules toggles, house rules or progress, and it does not touch their in-progress battles.

When you finish, use the battle screen's own controls:

- Record or auto-resolve the result to see the **results screen**.
- **Return** abandons the battle and takes you back to the setup screen.

## The Results Screen

The title says **VICTORY**, **HELD THE FIELD** or **DEFEAT**. Below it are:

- whether the objective was achieved, where the mission has one,
- rounds fought, casualties and injuries,
- enemies defeated, where the battle recorded it,
- a note if the battle was auto-resolved.

The screen ends with the reminder "Nothing from a Simulator battle is saved."

Two buttons follow:

- **Play Again.** Returns to the setup screen with the same settings and rolls a new battle from them.
- **Main Menu.** Returns to the Main Menu.

## Saving, Loading and Other Modes

- **Saving:** the Simulator has no save file, and the results screen says nothing is saved.
- **Loading:** there is nothing to load.
- **Characters:** the Simulator crew is generated fresh each time. There is no way to bring in a character from a campaign or send one out.
