# Chapter 7: Post-Battle Sequence

> **Quick Start** (for tabletop veterans)
> - 14 sequential steps resolve automatically with your input at key points
> - Pay is 1D6 credits plus the modifiers on Core Rules p.120
> - Holding the Field allows a Battlefield Finds roll (Core Rules p.120)
> - Injuries use the D100 Injury Table (Core Rules p.122); Bots use the Bot Injury Table
> - XP: +1 casualty, +2 survived without winning, +3 survived and won, plus bonuses (Core Rules p.123)

## Overview

After every battle, the app walks you through a 14-step post-battle sequence. Most steps resolve automatically based on your battle results, but several require decisions from you. This is where your crew gets paid, finds loot, treats injuries, gains experience, and deals with campaign events.

The Post-Battle Sequence runs in a fixed order. You can't skip steps, but some resolve instantly if they don't apply to your situation.

## The 14 Steps

### Step 1: Resolve Rival Status (Core Rules p.119)

- If you fought an opponent that is not a Rival and Held the Field, roll 1D6. On a 1 the opponents become your Rivals.
- If you fought an existing Rival and Held the Field, roll 1D6, adding +1 if you Tracked them down and +1 if you killed a Unique Individual. On a 4 or better they have had enough and you remove them from your Rivals list.
- This step is skipped for Invasion battles and for opponents from the Roving Threats subtable.

### Step 2: Resolve Patron Status (Core Rules p.119)

If you succeeded in a Patron mission, you may add the Patron to your contacts on this planet, unless the job was a One-time Contract. When you travel to a new planet, all Patrons become unavailable unless they are Persistent.

### Step 3: Determine Quest Progress (Core Rules p.120)

If the battle was part of a Quest, roll a D6, adding +1 for each Quest Rumor you hold and -2 if you did not Win.

- 3 or less: a dead end, and the Quest continues
- 4, 5 or 6: you are a step closer and gain a Quest Rumor
- 7 or more: you are at the conclusion, and the next Quest mission is the finale

If the modified roll was 4 or higher, roll another D6; on a 5-6 the next step is on another world.

### Step 4: Get Paid (Core Rules p.120)

You earn **1D6 credits**. Add +1 on Easy mode. If you won by completing your objective, treat a roll of 1 or 2 as a 3 (Rival missions get no bonus for winning). A Patron job adds its pay bonus. After the final mission of a Quest you roll twice, pick the better and add +1. An Invasion battle pays nothing.

### Step 5: Battlefield Finds (Core Rules pp.120-121)

If you Held the Field, roll D100 on the Battlefield Finds Table. You cannot roll after an Invasion battle. Results range from a slain enemy's weapon, usable goods and a Quest Rumor to a starship part, a personal trinket, debris, vital info, or nothing.

### Step 6: Check for Invasion (Core Rules p.121)

If the enemy you fought is an Invasion Threat, roll 2D6, adding +1 for Invasion Evidence, -1 if you Held the Field, +2 on Hardcore and +3 on Insanity. On a **9+** the world is about to be Invaded and you must follow the Flee Invasion step next turn (Core Rules p.69).

### Step 7: Gather Loot (Core Rules p.121)

Roll once on the Loot Table (Core Rules p.131). Some circumstances give extra rolls: after the final stage of a Quest you roll three times. An Invasion battle gives no loot.

Loot is added to your ship stash. You will distribute it at the next World Phase Assign Equipment step.

### Step 8: Determine Injuries and Recovery (Core Rules pp.121-122)

Any character who became a casualty rolls on the **Injury Table** (Bots and Soulless use the **Bot Injury Table**). A character who was merely knocked out by 3 simultaneous Stun results needs no roll.

| Roll (D100) | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| 1-5 | Gruesome fate: dead, and all carried equipment is damaged |
| 6-15 | Death or permanent injury: dead, or removed from the campaign |
| 16 | Miraculous escape: survives with +1 Luck, but all carried items are permanently lost |
| 17-30 | Equipment loss: a random carried item is damaged |
| 31-45 | Crippling wound: 1D6 credits of surgery or -1 permanent to the higher of Speed or Toughness; 1D6 campaign turns in Sick Bay |
| 46-54 | Serious injury: 1D3+1 campaign turns in Sick Bay |
| 55-80 | Minor injuries: 1 campaign turn in Sick Bay |
| 81-95 | Knocked out: no long-term effect |
| 96-100 | School of hard knocks: earn 1 XP |

A character with Luck who would be killed by this table survives but loses all Luck points. Damaged equipment cannot be used until it is repaired (Repair Your Kit crew task, Core Rules p.78).

### Step 9: Experience and Upgrades

Each character that took part in the battle earns XP (Core Rules p.123). Bots do not receive XP.

| Source | XP |
|--------|-----|
| Became a casualty | +1 |
| Survived, but did not win | +2 |
| Survived and won | +3 |
| First character to inflict a casualty | +1 |
| Killed a Unique Individual | +1 |
| Campaign is on Easy mode | +1 |
| Crew completed the final stage of a Quest | +1 |

A character that flees the battlefield in the first 2 rounds receives no XP. If a character has enough XP you may spend it on a Character Upgrade at this point. Costs and maximums are in {{chapter:08}}.

### Step 10: Advanced Training

You may send a crew member on an Advanced Training course (Core Rules pp.124-125): pay a 1 credit application fee, roll 2D6 for a 4+, then pay the course cost with XP, credits or both. The full course list and costs are in {{chapter:08}}. Only one attempt is permitted per campaign turn.

### Step 11: Purchase Items

Buy and sell equipment (Core Rules p.125):

- Pay 3 credits for one roll on the Military Weapon, Gear or Gadget table
- Buy Hand Guns, Blades, Colony Rifles or Shotguns for 1 credit each
- Sell up to 3 items for 1 credit each

See {{chapter:10}} for the panel.

### Step 12: Campaign Event

Roll D100 on the Campaign Events table and apply the result immediately (Core Rules p.125; the table is on pp.126-128):

- 28 results, from a friendly doc and a cargo sale to the tax man, damaged equipment and mouthing off to the wrong people
- Some results add a Quest Rumor, a Patron or a Rival
- The event and its consequence are shown on screen

### Step 13: Character Event

One random crew member (not a Bot or Soulless) rolls a personal event (Core Rules p.126):

- Development opportunities: gain contacts, learn secrets
- Complications: addictions, romantic entanglements, personal rivalries
- These events add narrative depth and sometimes mechanical effects

### Step 14: Galactic War Check

The final step tracks the state of the galaxy:

- Check if any planets have been invaded
- Update invasion status for worlds you've visited
- Track the overall galactic conflict state

This is mostly bookkeeping — the app handles it automatically.

## Post-Battle Tips

- **Pay for medical care.** At the World Phase you can pay 4 credits to remove 1 campaign turn from a character's Sick Bay time (Core Rules p.76).
- **Don't spend all your credits.** Keep a reserve for upkeep costs next turn.
- **Check loot carefully.** Implants found as loot are automatically installed (up to 2 per character for most species) — see {{chapter:09}}.
- **Read campaign events.** They often set up future opportunities or threats.
- **Track your XP.** Save it for expensive stat upgrades rather than spending small amounts frequently.
