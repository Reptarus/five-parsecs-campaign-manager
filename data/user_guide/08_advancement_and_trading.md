# Chapter 8: Advancement and Trading

> **Quick Start** (for tabletop veterans)
> - Stat upgrade costs: Combat/Reaction 7 XP, Speed/Savvy 5 XP, Toughness 6 XP, Luck 10 XP
> - Bots pay credits equal to the XP cost instead, once per ability (Core Rules p.123)
> - Advanced Training: 1 credit application, 2D6 for 4+, then the course cost (Core Rules pp.124-125)
> - Purchase Items: 3 credits per table roll, 1 credit for basic weapons, sell up to 3 items for 1 credit each (Core Rules p.125)

## Overview

Advancement and Trading are the two phases where you invest in your crew's long-term growth. Advancement lets you spend XP on stat improvements and new skills. Trading, at post-battle Step 11, lets you buy and sell equipment to keep your crew well-armed.

## The Advancement Phase

### Spending XP

Each stat has a per-point upgrade cost:

| Stat | Cost per +1 | Maximum |
|------|------------|-------------|
| Reactions | 7 XP | 6 |
| Combat Skill | 7 XP | +5 |
| Speed | 5 XP | 8" |
| Savvy | 5 XP | +5 |
| Toughness | 6 XP | 6 |
| Luck | 10 XP | 1 (3 for Humans) |

No ability may exceed its maximum, and Engineers cannot raise Toughness above 4 (Core Rules p.123). Species have their own starting values; see {{chapter:09}}.

### Bot Upgrades

Bots do not earn XP. A Bot may install an upgrade to any ability score by paying **credits equal to the XP cost** in the table above, and each ability score can be upgraded only once. Soulless use the normal XP process and cannot buy Bot upgrades (Core Rules p.123).

### Advanced Training

Advanced Training is bought at post-battle **Step 10** (Core Rules pp.124-125), in the **Advanced Training** dialog, not by repeating a crew task:

1. Select a crew member and pay a **1 credit** application fee.
2. Roll 2D6; a **4+** approves the application. On a failed roll you can try again later, but only one attempt is permitted per campaign turn.
3. Pay the course cost, using unspent XP, credits, or any combination.

| Course | Cost |
|--------|------|
| Pilot Training | 20 |
| Mechanic Training | 15 |
| Medical School | 20 |
| Merchant School | 10 |
| Security Training | 10 |
| Broker Training | 15 |
| Bot Technician | 10 |

Each crew member can only ever be trained in a single course, and you cannot benefit from more than one crew member with the same training. Bots pay the course cost in credits only (Core Rules p.124). The effect of each course is printed on Core Rules p.125 and shown in the dialog.

### Decision Guidance

- **Luck is the most expensive stat** (10 XP) but extremely powerful — rerolls save lives
- **Combat and Reaction** are the most impactful combat stats — prioritize for frontline fighters
- **Savvy** helps with non-combat tasks and events
- **Save XP for big upgrades** rather than spending 1-2 XP on minor gains each turn
- **XP also pays for Advanced Training.** Unspent XP can cover part of a course cost, so you do not have to choose between the two (Core Rules p.124)

## Trading: Purchase Items

Buying and selling is post-battle **Step 11, Purchase Items** (Core Rules p.125). It is covered in {{chapter:10}}. In short:

- Pay **3 credits** for a roll on the Military Weapon, Gear or Gadget table.
- Buy any number of Hand Guns, Blades, Colony Rifles or Shotguns for **1 credit** each.
- Sell up to **3 items** for **1 credit** each.

There is no tech-level marketplace, no condition-based pricing and no repair market in the rulebook, so the app has none.

### Trading Tips

- **Sell duplicates.** You do not need three of the same weapon, but you can only sell 3 items per turn.
- **Keep a rainy-day fund.** Do not spend all credits on equipment; upkeep costs come next turn.
